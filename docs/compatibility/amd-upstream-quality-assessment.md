## Multi-view: three different capabilities

1. **Official model:** TRELLIS.2-4B is documented as single-image conditioned. Images rendered from many training views do not establish simultaneous calibrated multi-view inference. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B)
2. **Installed fork:** real inference-only multi-image code already exists. `get_cond` concatenates or averages per-image embeddings; `run_multiview` accepts named front/back/left/right views and blends per-view predictions using spatial voxel weights with the same model slots. This does **not require retraining merely to run**, but it is not evidence of a model trained to fuse calibrated views. Concatenation, averaging and spatial blending are different algorithms. The qualified Modly adapter still admits one image and exact [1,1029,1024] conditioning. [AMD fork source](https://github.com/dmonkman/ComfyUI-Trellis2-AMD/blob/main/trellis2/pipelines/trellis2_image_to_3d.py)
3. **Trained camera-aware multi-view:** Pixal3D uses pixel-aligned projection and separate `*_mv` weights/`pipeline_mv.json`; this is a different model/conditioning integration, not passing a list into the current checkpoint. [Pixal3D documentation](https://github.com/TencentARC/Pixal3D#multi-view-inference)

The installed spatial sampler loops over views. With the same 12-step CFG schedule, two views imply approximately **42 shape forwards rather than the admitted 21** (and 44 rather than 22 occupancy forwards), before optional extras. Concatenation instead increases conditioning length; averaging loses explicit per-view identity. None is an approved free upgrade. Preserve existing caps; do not enable wrapper defaults that fill occupancy holes or keep only a shell. Consistent views of one pose/scale are necessary; a collage is not a substitute for calibrated input.

| Rank | Improvement / expected value | Implementation effort | Memory cost on RX 9070 XT 16 GB |
|---|---|---|---|

Different images can change sparse support, so the 4,430-token and decoder row limits still apply. |
| **2** | **One Shape-SLat guidance A/B, 7.5 → 5.0.** Low-cost, uncertain quality hypothesis: reduce extrapolative guidance while keeping the rest fixed; it may reduce artifacts or may lose reference fidelity. | Low-to-medium: a separately reviewed, versioned sampler contract/control is needed because 7.5 is pinned in both policy and control. Leave originals/decoder unchanged. | Same input dimensions and 21 model calls; similar expected shape-flow peak, not a measured guarantee. Learned decoder support may change and must pass unchanged caps. |

For fingers/toes, show separated silhouettes and inter-digit gaps rather than overlapping digits. For faces, supply visible form and enough face pixels; full-body conditioning cannot be assumed to preserve small facial detail. For wires/blades/leaves, preserve matte/negative space and avoid subpixel or occluded structures. For wounds, show actual rim/wall depth rather than only a painted dark mark. These are input-information hypotheses, not guarantees about unseen anatomy. They apply to props and other asset types as well as characters.

## What is established, and what is not

The publisher explicitly lists small holes/discontinuities and an unaligned foundation model as known limitations. That makes model limitations plausible, but does not attribute this zombie's individual defects exclusively to pretrained weights. AMD precision adaptations, upstream representation/assembly policy and input ambiguity are not separated by a matched upstream-device comparison. Qualification establishes bounded execution and contract fidelity, not universal NVIDIA/AMD perceptual parity. The accepted raw-quad verification rules out the measured CPU triangulation/sorting step as the origin of those specific excess incidences. [Known limitations](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B#known-limitations), [local converter evidence](amd-zombie-topology-diagnosis-verification.json)

Higher resolution could help small structures, but 1024 conditioning has 4× the spatial tokens; sparse support and cascade stages can also grow. Official standard requirements specify at least 24 GB NVIDIA VRAM, while Modly's proven 16 GB AMD route is staged/bounded 512. A 1024/1536 option, more steps, RK/Heun, DINO-lock or changed occupancy thresholds is not currently qualified. More steps are not an evidence-backed fix and would exceed the current call schedule. [Official configuration](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B/blob/main/pipeline.json), [requirements](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2#prerequisites)

Alternative components were assessed, not installed or recommended as immediate replacements. Pixal3D is MIT-labelled and offers trained multi-view, but requires new weights/projection conditioning and CUDA-oriented NATTEN; 16 GB RX 9070 XT compatibility is unverified. Hunyuan3D-2 reports 6 GB shape generation and provides smaller/trained multi-view variants, but that is not an AMD qualification; its community license explicitly excludes UK/EU/South Korea, so it is not a straightforward internationally usable Modly default. Neither is evidence of better results on our asset set. [Pixal3D requirements](https://github.com/TencentARC/Pixal3D#installation), [Hunyuan models](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2#-models-zoo), [license text](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/blob/main/LICENSE)
